Houston's big shows cluster at two venues: the **George R. Brown Convention Center** downtown (Comicpalooza, Anime Matsuri, Dream Con) and **NRG Center** (AnimeVerse). The calendar runs from AnimeVerse in February through a heavy late-spring and summer stretch.

Summer cons here mean serious heat and humidity. Plan cosplay, lines, and parking around it.
