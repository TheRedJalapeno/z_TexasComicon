Downtown San Antonio's Henry B. González Convention Center, right on the River Walk, hosts both **San Japan** (Labor Day weekend) and **Big Texas Comicon** (October). North on I-35, **Hill Country Comicon** keeps things community-sized in New Braunfels, and in the Rio Grande Valley, **South Texas Comic Con** is McAllen's big spring weekend.
