DFW has the deepest convention calendar in Texas. **FAN EXPO Dallas** brings the celebrity-heavy multi-fandom show downtown. **Texas Frightmare Weekend** is a national destination for horror fans. **Anime Frontier** closes out the year in Fort Worth, and **QuakeCon** turns the Gaylord Texan into one of the world's biggest LAN parties every August.

Most DFW venues sit along the DFW Airport–Las Colinas–Grapevine corridor, so out-of-town fans can often fly in and skip the rental car.
