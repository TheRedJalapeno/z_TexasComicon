Central Texas opens the convention year with **Ikkicon** in January and hosts the state's biggest retro gaming show, **Classic Game Fest**, every July. Up the road, **AggieCon** at Texas A&M is the world's longest-running student-run fan convention, and **Bell County Comic Con** offers a family-friendly summer weekend between Austin and Waco.
