West Texas fans don't have to drive to DFW. **El Paso Comic Con** draws from three states and two countries, **Yellow City Comic Convention** is the Panhandle's largest fandom event, and **Hub City Comic Con** brings voice actors and itasha cars to Lubbock each August.

Remember that El Paso runs on Mountain time.
