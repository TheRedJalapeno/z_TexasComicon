Texas Comicon readers are fans planning trips: checking dates, comparing shows, and clicking through to buy badges. That makes this a good fit for conventions, vendors, artists, hotels, and pop culture brands.

## Our rules

- **Dates and links are never for sale.** Sponsors get visibility, not editorial control over facts.
- **Everything paid is labeled.** Sponsored listings and posts carry a visible "Sponsored" tag, and their outbound links use `rel="sponsored"`.
- **Outbound links are tagged.** Every link to a convention includes UTM parameters (`utm_source=texascomicon`), so organizers can see the traffic we send in their own analytics.
