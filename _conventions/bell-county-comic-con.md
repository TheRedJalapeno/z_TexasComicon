Bell County Comic Con is a two-day, family-friendly expo in Belton, between Austin and Waco. It brings comics, pop culture, gaming, movies, and fantasy under one roof, with artists, vendors, cosplay contests, celebrity guests, and panels. It's a good pick for families around Killeen, Temple, and Fort Hood who want a full con experience without driving into a big city.

## When it happens

The show is an **early August** weekend at the Bell County Expo Center complex. The official site lists **August 7–8, 2027** and says 2027 tickets open January 1, 2027. The organizer's site refers to the venue by both its older name (Cadence Bank Center) and a newer one (Huntington Bank Expo). The address, 300 W Loop 121, is the same either way.

## Tips

- Doors are typically 10 a.m.–6 p.m. both days.
- August in Central Texas means triple-digit heat, so keep cosplay and kids hydrated walking in from the lot.
