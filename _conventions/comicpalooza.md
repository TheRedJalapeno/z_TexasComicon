Comicpalooza bills itself as "your pop-culture festival, curated for you, by people just like you." It's Houston's flagship multi-fandom convention, held at the George R. Brown Convention Center downtown. Expect a celebrity guest lineup, a large exhibitor floor and artist alley, special attractions, and a full programming schedule.

## When it happens

Comicpalooza lands on or near **Memorial Day weekend**. The 2026 show ran May 22–24. Ticketing sites list **May 28–30, 2027** for the next edition, but the official site hadn't published dates when we checked, so treat them as unconfirmed.

## Tips

- The GRB connects to downtown hotels and the METRORail Green/Purple lines. Garage parking fills on Saturday.
- Late May in Houston is hot and humid, so plan cosplay and lines around the heat.
