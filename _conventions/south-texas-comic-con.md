South Texas Comic Con is the Rio Grande Valley's major pop culture event. It's a three-day weekend at the McAllen Convention Center built around cosplay, voice-actor and celebrity guests, comics, and a vendor and artist floor. For fans in the Valley it's the closest large-scale con without a drive to San Antonio or Houston.

## When it happens

The show runs in **late April**. The official site lists **April 23–25, 2027**. Tickets were marked "coming soon" when we checked.

## Tips

- The convention center has a large surface parking lot, but Saturday is the busy day, so arrive early.
- Voice-actor panels are a highlight here. Check the schedule for the panels you want and plan around them.
