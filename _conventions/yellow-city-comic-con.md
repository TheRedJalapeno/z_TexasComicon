Yellow City Comic Convention ("YC3") is a community-run, annual fandom event and the largest of its kind in the Texas Panhandle. It runs Friday through Sunday at the Amarillo Civic Center Complex, with cosplay, artists and authors, a vendor hall, celebrity guests, panels, and gaming. The organizers also frame it as a tourism boost for Amarillo and a way to give back to the local community.

## When it happens

Yellow City is an **April** show. The official site lists **April 23–25, 2027**. The 2026 edition ran April 17–19.

## Tips

- Friday is an evening-only session, a good low-key way to preview the vendor hall.
- For fans in Lubbock and Oklahoma, it's the main spring con within a few hours' drive.
