Anime North Texas, "The DFW Anime Convention," is a summer hotel-style anime con at the Westin Dallas Fort Worth Airport, which also serves as the official convention hotel. Hotel cons have a different feel from the convention-center giants: tighter, social, and full of late-night programming. ANT also has a mobile app (iOS and Android) for the schedule.

## When it happens

Anime North Texas runs in **early August**. The 2026 edition was August 7–9. The 2027 dates haven't been announced.

## Tips

- Staying at the Westin puts you steps from panels. Rooms usually go fast once the block opens.
- It's right by DFW Airport, an easy fly-in con for out-of-state fans.
