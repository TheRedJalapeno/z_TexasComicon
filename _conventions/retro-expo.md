Retro Expo's tagline is "By collectors. For collectors." It's a weekend buy/sell/trade show for vintage toys, comics, video games, and related collectibles such as Funko Pops, records, and anime merch, with cosplayers in the aisles. It moved from the North Dallas Marriott to the larger Plano Event Center, and parking is free.

## When it happens

The show runs in **spring**. The 2026 edition was March 7–8, and the official site lists **April 17–18, 2027** (Saturday 10 a.m.–6 p.m., Sunday 11 a.m.–5 p.m.). Tickets weren't on sale yet when we checked.

## Tips

- Bring cash for vendors. Many accept cards, but cash gets better deals.
- Saturday morning is when rare stock moves. Sunday afternoon is when prices soften.
