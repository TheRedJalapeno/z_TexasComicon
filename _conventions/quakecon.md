QuakeCon is the long-running gaming festival from Bethesda and id Software, famous for its massive **bring-your-own-computer (BYOC) LAN**, where thousands of players set up rigs and game around the clock. Around the LAN you'll find tournaments, developer panels, sponsor exhibits, and giveaways. It has been a fixture at the Gaylord Texan in Grapevine for years.

## When it happens

QuakeCon is an **early-to-mid August** event, usually Thursday through Sunday. The 2026 edition ran August 6–9. The 2027 dates haven't been announced.

## Tips

- BYOC seats are reserved through a registration window and go quickly. Watch the official site in late spring.
- Book the Gaylord early or look at Grapevine hotels with shuttles. The resort sells out QuakeCon week.
