El Paso Comic Con is the largest pop culture convention in far West Texas, drawing fans from El Paso, Las Cruces, and Ciudad Juárez to the El Paso Convention Center downtown. Its tagline is "Fly Your Nerd Flag High!" The weekend mixes comics, cosplay, celebrity and creator guests, and a big local artist and vendor community.

## When it happens

The show is moving earlier in the year. The 2026 edition ran May 1–3, and the official site lists **March 5–7, 2027** for the next edition. If you're used to a May show, update your calendar.

## Tips

- El Paso is in the Mountain time zone, an hour behind most of Texas. Double-check panel times if you're driving in.
- The convention center is next to the Plaza Theatre and San Jacinto Plaza, with plenty of food within walking distance.
