AnimeVerse Fest Houston, produced by Zimmad Events, fills NRG Center each February with celebrity guests, gaming, panels, a cosplay contest, live main-stage performances, and more than a hundred vendors. It's the biggest winter anime weekend on the Gulf Coast and a nice counterweight to Houston's summer-heavy convention calendar.

## When it happens

AnimeVerse is a **mid-February** show, usually Valentine's Day weekend. The official site lists **February 12–14, 2027** at NRG Center.

## Tips

- NRG Park has massive paid parking lots. METRORail's Red Line also stops at Stadium Park/Astrodome.
- Card graders (CGC/JSA) have taken on-site submissions at past shows. Check announcements if you collect.
