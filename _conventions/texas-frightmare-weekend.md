Texas Frightmare Weekend is the state's flagship horror convention and one of the best-known horror cons in the country. It celebrated its 20th anniversary in 2026. Expect a deep lineup of horror film and TV guests, screenings, panels, and a vendor floor full of genre art, props, and collectibles. After years at the Hyatt Regency DFW (2012–2022), it moved to the Irving Convention Center at Las Colinas.

## When it happens

Frightmare runs in **late May**. The 2026 edition was May 22–24. Event listings show **May 21–23, 2027** for the next edition, but we haven't seen it on the official site yet, so treat it as unconfirmed.

## Tips

- Autograph and photo-op lines for headliners can be long. Many fans buy add-ons in advance.
- The convention center is next to the Toyota Music Factory, which has plenty of food between sessions.
