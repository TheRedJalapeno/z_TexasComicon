Ikkicon calls itself "Central Texas' Premier Anime + Gaming Con." It has been running for more than 20 years and kicks off the Texas convention year every January. The show combines anime programming, gaming, cosplay, evening and nightlife events, and community meetups. Since moving to the Kalahari Resort in Round Rock, just north of Austin, it has a self-contained resort feel.

## When it happens

Ikkicon runs the **second weekend of January**. The official site lists **January 8–10, 2027**.

## Tips

- Staying at the Kalahari keeps you inside for late-night programming. The resort's water park is a bonus for families.
- January weather in Central Texas swings from 30s to 70s, so pack layers for cosplay.
