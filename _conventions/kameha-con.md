Kameha Con bills itself as the world's largest fan gathering and unofficial Dragon Ball convention. It's a single-franchise show that goes deep: Dragon Ball guests, vendors, cosplay, after-hours parties, bracketed tournaments run on start.gg, and a "ring opening ceremony" for ring buyers. Its 2026 edition (its 7th year) brought it back to Texas, at Freeman Expo Hall in San Antonio with the AC Hotel by Marriott as host hotel.

## When it happens

The 2026 show ran **August 21–23**. Kameha Con has moved cities before, so watch the official site for the 2027 location and dates.

## Tips

- VIP badges get early floor entry (10 a.m. vs. 10:30 a.m. GA in 2026).
- Tournament sign-ups run through start.gg. Register early if you're competing.
