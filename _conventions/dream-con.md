Dream Con was founded by the YouTube collective RDCWorld. It's a fast-growing convention that blends anime, gaming, sports, film, music, and creator culture, with an explicit focus on inclusivity and showcasing diverse talent. It has made the George R. Brown Convention Center in Houston its home, and its creator-driven stage shows and tournaments draw fans from across the country.

## When it happens

Dream Con is a **summer** show. The 2026 edition ran July 10–12. The official site lists **June 18–20, 2027**, a few weeks earlier than 2026.

## Tips

- Creator meet-and-greets and stage shows are the main draw. Watch the official socials for schedule drops and add-on sales.
- June in Houston: hydrate, and use the GRB's skybridge to the Hilton-Americas for air-conditioned breaks.
