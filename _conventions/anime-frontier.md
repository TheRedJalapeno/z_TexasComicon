Anime Frontier is produced by LeftField Media, the company behind Anime NYC, and presented by Crunchyroll. Since launching in Fort Worth it has become the year-end anime event in North Texas. Expect industry-heavy programming: Japanese publisher and studio presence, premieres and announcements, a large exhibit hall and artist alley, and plenty of cosplay in downtown Fort Worth.

## When it happens

Anime Frontier runs in **early-to-mid December** at the Fort Worth Convention Center. The 2025 show ran December 12–14. Multiple event listings show **December 4–6, 2026**, but the official site hadn't updated when we checked, so confirm there before you book.

## Tips

- The convention center is walkable to Sundance Square, with plenty of food and a good spot for holiday photos.
- Industry panels with premieres can cap out. Check whether your badge tier includes reserved seating.
