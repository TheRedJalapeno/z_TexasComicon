AggieCon started in 1969 and bills itself as the longest-running student-run fan convention in the world. Texas A&M students organize it on the second floor of the Memorial Student Center. It has grown into an anime-forward fan con with a dealers' room, artist alley, TCG and video game tournaments, a cosplay contest, panels, and performances. 2026 was its 55th edition.

## When it happens

The dates float between **late January and early March**. 2026 ran January 31–February 1, and the official site lists **March 6–7, 2027**.

## Tips

- Campus parking: the Central Campus Garage is closest to the MSC. Weekend rates apply.
- It's an inexpensive, low-pressure con, a great first convention for Brazos Valley teens.
- AggieCon's site moved to **aggiecon.net**. Older listings pointing to .org are out of date.
