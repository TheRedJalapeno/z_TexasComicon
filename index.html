Your guide to Texas fan conventions

Texas hosts more fan conventions than almost any other state: more than a hundred a year, from 500-person library cons to 60,000-fan weekends. This guide focuses on the established shows worth planning a trip around, across four kinds of events:

  • Comic & pop culture cons, such as FAN EXPO Dallas, Comicpalooza, and Big Texas Comicon: celebrity guests, comics creators, artist alleys, and cosplay.
  • Anime conventions, such as San Japan, Anime Frontier, Anime Matsuri, and Ikkicon: industry panels, voice actors, J-fashion, concerts, and late-night programming.
  • Horror, led by Texas Frightmare Weekend, one of the country's best-known horror conventions.
  • Gaming & retro, including QuakeCon's bring-your-own-computer LAN, Classic Game Fest, and Retro Expo.

How we verify dates

Every listing shows one of three labels. Confirmed means the dates are published on the organizer's official website. Unconfirmed means ticketing or calendar sites list dates the organizer hasn't posted yet. Dates TBA means the next edition hasn't been announced, so we show when the last one ran. Each page carries the date we last checked it.
