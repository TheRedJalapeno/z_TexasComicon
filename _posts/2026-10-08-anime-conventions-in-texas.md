Texas has one of the busiest anime convention calendars in the country, with a major show almost every month of the year. Here's how the big ones differ, in calendar order.

## Winter

**[Ikkicon]({{ '/conventions/ikkicon/' | relative_url }}), January, Round Rock.** Central Texas's premier anime + gaming con has run for more than 20 years. Now based at the Kalahari Resort, it has a self-contained resort feel and strong nightlife programming.

**[AnimeVerse Houston]({{ '/conventions/animeverse-houston/' | relative_url }}), February, Houston.** A big NRG Center show with celebrity guests, gaming, main-stage performances, and 100+ vendors, usually on Valentine's Day weekend.

**[AggieCon]({{ '/conventions/aggiecon/' | relative_url }}), late winter/early spring, College Station.** The world's longest-running student-run fan convention, now anime-forward. It's affordable and a great first con.

## Summer

**[Dream Con]({{ '/conventions/dream-con/' | relative_url }}), summer, Houston.** Founded by RDCWorld, Dream Con mixes anime with gaming, sports, music, and creator culture, and puts inclusivity at the center.

**[Anime Matsuri]({{ '/conventions/anime-matsuri/' | relative_url }}), summer, Houston.** One of the largest anime and Japanese pop culture conventions in the South, known for J-fashion, concerts, and a season of free community events leading up to it. It hit its 20th anniversary in 2026.

**[Anime North Texas]({{ '/conventions/anime-north-texas/' | relative_url }}), August, Irving.** "The DFW Anime Convention" is a hotel-style con at the Westin DFW Airport. It's social and runs late.

**[Kameha Con]({{ '/conventions/kameha-con/' | relative_url }}), August, San Antonio (2026).** The world's largest unofficial Dragon Ball fan convention. It's a single-franchise show, with tournaments and after-hours events.

## Fall

**[San Japan]({{ '/conventions/san-japan/' | relative_url }}), Labor Day weekend, San Antonio.** The largest anime and gaming convention in South Texas is fan-run and fills the convention center and Grand Hyatt by the River Walk.

**[Anime Frontier]({{ '/conventions/anime-frontier/' | relative_url }}), December, Fort Worth.** Presented by Crunchyroll and produced by the Anime NYC team, Anime Frontier is the most industry-heavy anime show in Texas, with publisher and studio presence and announcements.

## Which one should you pick?

- **Want industry news and premieres?** Anime Frontier.
- **Want the biggest party?** San Japan or Anime Matsuri.
- **Want a resort weekend?** Ikkicon.
- **Want something small and cheap?** AggieCon.
